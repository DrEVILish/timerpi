Venue mesh radio

The boxes mesh over Wi-Fi (network timerpi, 20 MHz). The built-in Wi-Fi starts on 2.4 GHz at every boot; a USB Wi-Fi adapter adds the other band. Changes apply at the next reboot.

CuTePi media bridge (OSC)

Pair a CuTePi media wall: cue starts fire its QLab channel (/cue/<pos>/start) and BLANK cuts it to the panic holding image. Off by default — enable only on a trusted control LAN (OSC carries no authentication). UDP has no ack: the test button proves the packet left, never that the peer acted.
